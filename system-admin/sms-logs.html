SMS Log

Every text message the system tried to send, and whether it worked.

Sent means the SMS gateway accepted the message and queued it on the phone that sends your texts. It is not a delivery receipt, so a text can still fail to reach the resident if that phone is offline or the number is wrong. Failed means the gateway refused it or could not be reached; the reason is shown under the message.
